Core 300S status frames with CMD 01 32 40 (19-byte payload)

Unit:     LAP-C302S-WGC (region AE), stock ESP32-SOLO-1C module
MCU:      reports 1.0.0 (payload bytes 0..2 = 00 00 01)
Stock FW: ESP32 image built February 2025; its log strings name 01 32 40 as the
          status report and 01 33 40 as purifier_uart_query_device_status

Source: NOT a logic analyser capture. These are payloads logged by replacement
firmware on the ESP32 (github.com/tadhglewis/levoit-matter), which hex-dumps the
payload of each frame it receives from the MCU. Every line is the payload
(frame bytes 10..28) of a frame of the form

    A5 22 <seq> 17 00 <chk> 01 32 40 00 <19 payload bytes>

sent by the MCU. This unit never sends 01 30 40 or 01 B0 40. It pushes status
once a minute and on every change. It acks the documented status request
01 31 40 with an empty payload, and answers 01 33 40 with an ack (0x12)
carrying this same 19-byte payload.

The state named on each line is what the purifier was doing, as set from its
own panel or by command.

off, selected speed 1, display setting on
    00 00 01 00 00 01 00 00 01 FF 00 01 05 00 00 00 00 00 01

off, selected speed 3, display setting off
    00 00 01 00 00 03 00 00 00 FF 00 01 05 00 00 00 00 00 00

off, mode byte still auto
    00 00 01 00 02 01 00 00 01 FF 00 01 01 00 00 00 00 00 01

manual speed 1, display on
    00 00 01 01 00 01 00 64 01 01 00 01 01 00 00 00 00 00 00

manual speed 1, display on, PM2.5 5
    00 00 01 01 00 01 00 64 01 01 00 01 05 00 00 00 00 00 01

manual speed 1, display on, filter LED on
    00 00 01 01 00 01 00 64 01 01 01 01 01 00 00 00 00 00 00

manual speed 3, display on, filter LED on
    00 00 01 01 00 03 00 64 01 03 01 01 01 00 00 00 00 00 00

sleep, selected speed 3, filter LED on (backlight still lit, just after the change)
    00 00 01 01 01 03 00 64 01 00 01 01 01 00 00 00 00 00 00

sleep, same, backlight now off (display setting byte unchanged)
    00 00 01 01 01 03 00 00 01 00 01 01 01 00 00 00 00 00 00

auto, selected speed 1, clean air (running speed 0)
    00 00 01 01 02 01 00 64 01 00 00 01 01 00 00 00 00 00 00

auto, selected speed 2
    00 00 01 01 02 02 00 64 01 00 00 01 01 00 00 00 00 00 00

auto, selected speed 3, PM2.5 3
    00 00 01 01 02 03 00 64 01 00 00 01 03 00 00 00 00 00 00

Layout, compared with the 18-byte 01 30 40 status: identical through offset 5,
one byte inserted at offset 6, every later field one byte further on.

    0..2    MCU version: patch, minor, major
    3       power
    4       mode: 0 manual, 1 sleep, 2 auto
    5       selected manual speed 1..3 (kept while off and in auto)
    6       unknown, 00 in every frame
    7       display backlight right now: 00 / 64
    8       display setting: 00 off / 01 on
    9       running speed: the speed in manual, 00 in sleep and in auto with
            clean air, FF when off
    10      filter LED
    11      AQI 1..4
    12..13  PM2.5, LE16
    14      child lock (confirmed on hardware; no frame with it set is in this file)
    15      auto mode (by position; not exercised)
    16..17  efficiency area (by position; not exercised)
    18      00 / 01, meaning unconfirmed. Not an error: 01 was present while the
            purifier ran normally

With the display set to off, pressing any button lights the backlight for about
three seconds: offset 7 goes to 64 and back while offset 8 stays 00. Offset 8 is
the one that follows the display command 01 05 A1.
