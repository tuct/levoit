All of that is read from a full flash backup taken before the stock firmware was
replaced. The stock image's log strings name 01 32 40 as the status report and
01 33 40 as purifier_uart_query_device_status.

For comparison, this project lists MCU firmware 2.0.7 and 2.0.11 as tested on
the Core 300S, both on the 18-byte 01 30 40 status. This unit reports a lower
MCU version next to a 2025 build date, so it looks like a separate controller
line and not an earlier one. Whether that goes with the WGC / AE variant or
with the manufacturing date cannot be told from one unit.

Source: NOT a logic analyser capture. Replacement firmware on the ESP32
(github.com/tadhglewis/levoit-matter) logged every frame it received and every
frame it sent, as raw bytes, and the log was read over Wi-Fi. Times are seconds
since the ESP32 booted, at about 10 ms resolution, taken when the frame was
parsed or queued for sending. Every checksum below verifies.

In the first capture all state changes were made at the purifier's own panel.
The ESP32 sent no control commands during it: only acks, the Wi-Fi LED command at boot
and a status request every 5 s. That request and its reply are shown once and
left out after that; all of them were 01 33 40 answered as below.

This unit never sends 01 30 40 or 01 B0 40. It pushes 01 32 40 on every change
and once a minute. It acks a 01 31 40 status request with an empty payload and
answers 01 33 40 with the status (end of the second capture).

The ESP32's ack to a pushed frame is type 12 with its own sequence number and
byte 4 = FF. The MCU accepts it. Twice in this capture the MCU sent the same
frame again about 500 ms later with the same sequence number (E0, E1), both
times right after a mode change; cause not established.

Layout, compared with the 18-byte 01 30 40 status: identical through offset 5,
one byte inserted at offset 6, every later field one byte further on.

    0..2    MCU version: patch, minor, major
    3       power
    4       mode: 0 manual, 1 sleep, 2 auto
    5       selected manual speed 1..3 (kept while off, in sleep and in auto)
    6       unknown, 00 in every frame
    7       display backlight right now: 00 / 64
    8       display setting: 00 off / 01 on
    9       running speed: the speed in manual, 00 in sleep and in auto with
            clean air, FF when off
    10      filter LED (00 throughout this capture)
    11      AQI 1..4 (01 throughout this capture)
    12..13  PM2.5, LE16. Reads 5 while the purifier is off, whatever it read before
    14      child lock
    15      auto mode, meaning the preference and not the fan mode at offset 4:
            00 Default, 01 Quiet, 02 Room Size. Follows command 01 E6 A5
            (second capture)
    16..17  efficiency area, LE16, as sent in 01 E6 A5. Kept after the mode
            goes back to Default (second capture)
    18      00 throughout this capture. 01 was seen on other days in normal
            running, so it is not an error flag. Meaning unconfirmed
